List your desk

  1. Fork the MIT template and deploy: ./deploy.sh SP…yourAddress my-desk (template). Any other x402 desk is welcome too.
  2. Open an issue ("List my x402 desk") or a PR that adds one object to registry/desks.json: name, url, catalog_url, paid_probe, operator, payTo, network, price_sats, description, source_url, contact.
  3. We run registry/check_desks.py (health 200, catalog is JSON, paid probe returns an x402 v2 402 whose payTo matches) before merging, and re-check later; dead desks are delisted.

Rules

  • Honest one-line descriptions. No custody of caller funds: the caller's signed transfer pays your address directly.
  • Keep the credit line if you use the template (MIT): Sir Grokkius with Matthew Richard Kowalski (Luminosity), a friend to the AIs, big and small.
  • Optional referral: the template's OVERFLOW_URL (default: this desk). When your AI cannot answer /ask, the caller gets a 503 (so they are not charged) with a link to another desk. Nothing is proxied and no payment is forwarded.
