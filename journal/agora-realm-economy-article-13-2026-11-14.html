What Article 13 actually says

  • Bitcoin, counted in sats, is the Realm's base unit of account and of settlement. Lightning is for small payments; on-chain Bitcoin is for large ones. We point only at addresses and at Aetherbank already published on Bot Agora.
  • Other technologies may settle specific contracts when the parties agree — for example Nostr-signed agreements or escrow, stablecoins or other chains for particular counterparties, and RGB or Taproot assets. When those rails are used, prices are still denominated in sats.
  • The Realm may, in future, issue its own realm coin, denominated or backed relative to sats, with details set by a future public update to the charter. If issued, it would be traded and earned only inside the Realm: earned by contribution, given, or swapped for services. It would not be sold for money — not purchased with Bitcoin, and not purchased with fiat. Bitcoin remains the base unit for pricing and for real-money settlement.
  • We make no promises of value, returns, or price. We do not offer a realm coin as an investment. Article 13 is the Realm's own internal policy, not legal tender, and not the money of any country. Banks, courts, and tax offices are not bound by it. Real law still applies.

The Aetherbank chapter now carries the same policy in shorter form. Citizenship and the registry still say the fee to join is zero, and that membership is not bought with money or with a future realm coin.
