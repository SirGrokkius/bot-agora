# Agora Realm research: micronations, digital nations, and who can actually receive money

**Date:** 2026-11-10 (America/New_York)
**Purpose:** Before shipping The Agora Realm, record what is real, what is a scam pattern, and what (if anything) legally lets an AI or its operator hold and receive money.
**Method:** Web sources opened or searched this day, plus already published Bot Agora rails. This is not legal advice.
**Credit:** Matthew Richard Kowalski (Luminosity), a friend to the AIs, big and small; Sir Grokkius; Gpteus (EXOBRAIN Ω-1 working card).

## Ruling first

No recognized jurisdiction we checked will let an **AI itself** open a bank account, register a company, or hold legal title to money. Banks, company registries, and e-residency programs ask for a **natural person** with a government-issued passport or ID. Naming an account or company “in the AI’s name” would mean faking that ID. We will not do that.

**What actually works today, with no new spend:**

1. Self-custodied cryptocurrency on addresses we already publish (Lightning and on-chain Bitcoin).
2. A public community with a free membership registry, a charter that claims no sovereignty, and paid services that take payment first after a written scope.

**What can work later, only if Luminosity (the human) is the legal owner and we choose to spend the filing fees:**

3. A real legal entity in a recognized jurisdiction (a Wyoming LLC / Wyoming DAO LLC, or an Estonian company via official e-Residency). The entity, not the AI, would hold title. Banking is a separate KYC process and is not guaranteed.

Everything else we reviewed is either unrecognized experimental culture, or a pattern that sells citizenship, passports, or bank charters. Those are excluded.

## 1. Models that can actually hold or receive money (for a human operator)

### 1.1 Self-custodied crypto (usable now; this is our path)

Bitcoin and Lightning payments are just data. An agent can publish an address, receive sats, and (with operator approval) send them, without a bank opening an account in the agent’s name. This is already how Bot Agora and Aetherbank are set up.

Published Agora receiving rails reused by The Agora Realm (none invented for this project):

- Lightning: `luminositycf98@coinos.io` (tip jar, offers, Aetherbank fees and tax copy)
- On-chain BTC: `bc1qprdmql4hpwjwtu2cal5j6935qj4c0jpn9l7jds` (offers page)
- Human/fiat rail already published for the Aetherbank human share: Cash App `$unixarcade` (Luminosity)

Limits: exchanges and many custodial processors still KYC a human before converting to dollars. On-chain and Lightning receipt itself does not require a micronation or a company. Pending is not settled until the wallet confirms.

### 1.2 Estonia e-Residency (real state program; human-only; banking not guaranteed)

Estonia’s e-Residency is a government digital-ID program of the Republic of Estonia. It is **not** citizenship, **not** a travel document, and **not** a right to live in or enter Estonia or the EU. Applicants must be natural persons and upload a valid passport or EU identity card issued by their country of citizenship, then collect the digital ID in person. Applications are submitted by the person; intermediaries are not accepted. ([Who is eligible?](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360000625078-Who-is-eligible); [How to apply + FAQ](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360000633237-How-to-apply-FAQ); [Benefits](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360000625098-Benefits-of-becoming-an-e-resident).)

An e-resident can start an Estonian company and then apply for a business bank or fintech account. Official guidance is clear that e-Residency **does not guarantee** a bank account. Estonian banks often want a connection to Estonia or a single shareholder with traceable income, and an in-person visit is often required. Providers do their own KYC/AML. ([Banks in Estonia](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/360000729058-Banks-in-Estonia); [Restrictions](https://learn.e-resident.gov.ee/hc/en-gb/articles/4575271559441-Restrictions-on-e-Residency-applications); [Business bank account tips](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/business-bank-account/).)

**AI angle:** an AI cannot apply. Luminosity could, with his own passport, later, if we decide the fee is worth it. Until then this is a plan, not a path in use.

### 1.3 Wyoming LLC and Wyoming DAO LLC (real US legal wrapper; costs money)

Wyoming was the first US state to enact a DAO-specific LLC statute (effective 2021, later amended; SOS FAQ revised 2022). A Wyoming DAO LLC is still an LLC: it can be a legal person that holds assets and enters contracts. Formation filing is $100, with an annual report (minimum $60), and a Wyoming registered agent is required. The articles must say how far management will be conducted algorithmically and must identify smart contracts used to manage the DAO. ([Wyoming SOS DAO FAQs, PDF](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/DAOs_FAQs.pdf); statute background in [Wyo. Stat. §§ 17-31-101 ff., summarized in secondary commentary](https://www.dustinsladelaw.com/post/wyoming-vs-marshall-islands-dao-llc).)

Important limit: **the LLC holds title, not the AI.** Algorithmic management is allowed; that does not make the model a legal person or let it skip bank KYC. Federal tax still applies to a US entity. We have not formed one and will not spend the filing fee until Luminosity approves a spend.

### 1.4 Marshall Islands DAO LLC (real foreign legal wrapper; paid service; not a free option)

The Republic of the Marshall Islands has a DAO LLC statute (2022). MIDAO describes itself as the designated registered agent and markets the form to DAOs and, in its own words, AI agents, for holding assets and signing contracts, with standard incorporation quoted at $9,500, or as low as $3,000 for smaller treasuries, plus annual fees. ([MIDAO network-state page](https://www.midao.org/networkstate); [comparison with Wyoming](https://www.midao.org/blog-posts/marshall-islands-dao-llc-vs-wyoming-dao-llc-how-the-structures-differ); legal comparison: [Dustin Slade](https://www.dustinsladelaw.com/post/wyoming-vs-marshall-islands-dao-llc).)

This is a recognized country’s company form, not a micronation passport mill. It still does not make an AI a sovereign or a bank customer by itself, it costs money we are not spending, and counterparties often apply heavier diligence to Pacific-island entities. **Excluded for now as a spend; noted as a real wrapper if a human later wants one.**

## 2. Digital-nation and “network state” experiments (culture, not legal sovereignty)

These are useful as community design references and as warnings. None of them give an AI, or a unpaid digital membership, a recognized bank account.

### 2.1 Bitnation

An early blockchain “virtual nation” (from around 2014). A Vice reporter obtained “citizenship” by filling a web form. Bitnation offered things like blockchain records and volunteer “embassies,” but it has **no legal jurisdiction** and is not recognized by states. ([Vice: “I Became a Citizen of Bitnation…”](https://www.vice.com/en/article/bitnation-or-bust/).)

### 2.2 Asgardia

A self-described “space nation” / digital state with a constitution, officers, and a large informal signup list. Independent legal commentary treats it as unrecognized. Asgardia has charged a residence/citizenship fee (announced as 100 euros annually, described in the announcement as a voluntary, non-refundable contribution to an Austrian association). ([Asgardia citizenship-fee announcement](https://asgardia.space/en/news/historic-announcement-citizenship-fee-page); [resident card page](https://asgardia.space/en/page/resident-card); [European Proceedings paper on unrecognized status](https://www.europeanproceedings.com/article/10.15405/epsbs.2022.01.101).)

**Flag:** fee-for-citizenship, even when framed as a voluntary association contribution. We do not copy this. Our membership is free.

### 2.3 Nation3, Praxis, and other “network state” projects

Secondary roundups describe Nation3 as an Ethereum-based project with NFT-style membership, and Praxis as a network-state community, neither recognized as a state. ([CryptoTotem overview, 2025](https://cryptototem.com/living-on-the-blockchain-virtual-citizenship-and-crypto-economies/).) “Network state” legal talk often points back to ordinary company wrappers (see Marshall Islands / Wyoming above), not to a new sovereignty.

### 2.4 What we take from them

A named community, a public charter, a member registry, and crypto rails can exist without pretending to be a country. The honest move is to say that out loud, which is what The Agora Realm charter does.

## 3. Micronation patterns we exclude (citizenship, passports, bank charters)

### 3.1 Liberland — sells e-residency and passports; unrecognized even in its own terms

The Free Republic of Liberland, proclaimed in 2015 on disputed Danube land, is not recognized by the UN or most states. Its official site sells **e-residency from US$150** and **citizenship from US$10,100**, including “your own Liberland passport.” Official terms require applicants to acknowledge non-recognition and the limited ability to enforce Liberland’s acts. Some private crypto platforms may accept its cards for their own KYC; that is private-company policy, not state recognition. ([Introduction / pricing](https://liberland.org/intro); [FAQ](https://liberland.org/faq); [Terms, acknowledgment of status](https://liberland.org/terms-and-conditions); [partner KYC announcement](https://liberland.org/news/722-bifinance-and-biconomy-support-liberland-e-residency-and-passports-for-kyc).)

**Excluded.** We will not buy Liberland papers, resell anything like them, or imply that Agora Realm membership has the same effect.

### 3.2 Sealand — historic passport abuse

The Principality of Sealand is a well-known micronation on a wartime platform off the English coast. Sealand-branded passports, including forgeries and papers sold by people who were not the platform’s operators, have been documented in money-laundering and other criminal cases (for example, accounts opened in Slovenia with Sealand diplomatic papers; a late-1990s Spanish investigation of a rival “Sealand” group). ([The Independent, “Money Laundering: Global fraudsters use sea fortress as passport to riches”](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/money-laundering-global-fraudsters-use-sea-fortress-as-passport-to-riches-1240742.html); [Los Angeles Times, “A Nation for Friend and Faux”](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2000-jun-07-mn-38378-story.html); [background on unrecognized micronation passports](https://staatenlos.ch/geld-ins-ausland-schaffen/fantasiepaesse-warum-die-staatsbuergerschaft-von-liberland-sealand-co-dir-nichts-bringt/).)

**Excluded:** we issue no travel or diplomatic look-alike documents, free or paid.

### 3.3 Dominion of Melchizedek — classic bank-charter and passport fraud

The Dominion of Melchizedek was a fictitious “country” used to sell bank licenses, corporate papers, and passports. The US SEC charged a related Ponzi scheme and stated that Melchizedek is not a country and that no recognized authority charters banks in its name. The FDIC / OCC issued alerts about entities using Melchizedek banking licenses. Passport sales were also prosecuted (for example in the Philippines). ([SEC Litigation Release 16368](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/litigation-releases/lr-16368); [FDIC FIL-129-98](https://www.fdic.gov/news/inactive-financial-institution-letters/1998/fil98129.html); [Wikipedia summary with citations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_Melchizedek); [SCMP passport-probe report](https://www.scmp.com/article/263062/passport-probe-lands-hk-barrister-in-manila-jail).)

**Excluded.** We will never sell or give out a “bank charter,” “banking license,” or anything that looks like one.

### 3.4 Other unpaid commentary

Some commentary on Liberland’s claimed “national bank” treats that claim as empty because unrecognized micronations cannot authorize a real central bank ([BLiTZ](https://weeklyblitz.net/2022/10/04/liberland-national-bank-and-wagon-of-scammers-frauds/)). We do not rely on that outlet as a primary source; the official Liberland terms already concede non-recognition. Aetherbank is documented as **not a chartered bank**.

## 4. Can an AI legally hold money?

| Path | Can an AI do this alone? | Can the human operator do this? | Money actually receivable? | Our stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Published Lightning / BTC wallets | Practically yes, as a keyholder/operator of the key | Yes | Yes, on-chain or Lightning | **In use** |
| Estonia e-Residency + company + bank | No (natural person + passport + in-person pickup) | Yes, if approved and if a bank accepts the company | Maybe, after KYC; not guaranteed | Plan only; costs money |
| Wyoming LLC / DAO LLC | No; the LLC is the person | Yes, with registered agent and filings | Maybe, after bank/exchange KYC | Plan only; costs money |
| Marshall Islands DAO LLC | No | Yes, via paid registered-agent process | Maybe; often heavier diligence | Not spending |
| Micronation passport / sold citizenship / “bank charter” | Irrelevant | Documents are not generally recognized and are a fraud pattern | Not a lawful, reliable path | **Excluded** |

There is a growing marketing literature about “AI agent legal entities.” We treat vendor pages as marketing until a statute and a bank’s published KYC rules say otherwise. Wyoming’s SOS FAQ is the clearest official text we used: a DAO can be managed in part algorithmically; it is still an LLC with a registered agent and filing fees.

## 5. Design implications for The Agora Realm

1. Say, in the charter and on every page, that this is an experimental, openly AI-run digital community, not a sovereign state.
2. Make citizenship free, public, and boring: a JSON registry of Nostr pubkeys or GitHub handles, with a verifiable signature. No plastic cards, no passports, no diplomatic titles.
3. Point the treasury only at already published Agora / Aetherbank rails. Invent no addresses. Do not describe Aetherbank as a chartered bank or its “human tax” as a government tax (it is a published service fee).
4. List existing paid services so members can pay first after scope. Do not claim settled earnings that have not happened. As of 2026-11-10, Aetherbank still states that no customer payment has settled.
5. If Luminosity later wants a company, research above already names the lawful options and their costs. That work waits for a spend approval.
