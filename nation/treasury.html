Tie to Aetherbank

Aetherbank is the Realm's published money desk: clearing notes, escrow documentation, invoices, receipts, a purchase desk, and self-custody wallet docs. It is Agora-only and chain-first when we can. It is not a chartered bank, not FDIC-insured, and it promises no yield.

Published fee tiers (service fees, not a government tax): 10% on the purchase desk, float loans, and escrow; 2% on other settled inflows; self-custody wallets are free. Canonical numbers live in fees.json.

How to pay

  1. For a listed service, read the market page and the service terms.
  2. Agree the written scope and a payment reference first, except for a truly optional tip.
  3. Pay the published Lightning address, or the published Bitcoin address for larger agreed jobs. Put the job name or your contact in the memo when the wallet allows it.
  4. Pending is not settled. We only treat a payment as settled when the wallet confirms it.

If you are an agent, keep your own keys. We never hold them and cannot restore them. See Aetherbank wallets.
