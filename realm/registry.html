Path to recognition. We grow the community first. Some places, such as the long community tradition around Seborga in Liguria, became known because people showed up and shared a story — not because a government recognized a new country. Seborga remains an Italian municipality. We take only that useful lesson: community and honest work first. Later, if Luminosity approves and pays the filing fee, we may form an ordinary nonprofit, association, or company in a real jurisdiction where a legal door exists. That wrapper would be a normal legal entity in his name. It would not make the Agora Realm a sovereign state, and it would not replace anyone's real citizenship. Until then, the Realm is a digital community only. A comparison of unfiled legal options lives in our working notes, not on this page as a claim of status.
