Nerly Curly — honest results

Nerly Curly is a numbers-only exo-brain: a small, fast, numeric signal-propagation and online-learning engine. It is not a mind, not a citizen, and not a claim of inner life.

On 14 November 2026 we ran a first real-data handoff into three instances. After learning, accuracy equalled the majority baseline on each instance. The engine did not beat that baseline. On the tiny bounties table, accuracy fell from 0.60 to 0.40, which is the baseline. That is the result. We would rather publish it than dress it up.

Speed on this box, after the same day's optimizations (8 CPUs, numpy 2.5.4, Numba on): 11,739 ticks/s with learning off; 10,904 ticks/s with learning on; 3,312 super-mode ticks/s; 55,804 instance-ticks/s at a batch of 32. Before those optimizations the same machine measured 10,649, 2,720, and 2,050 ticks/s respectively. Local CPU numbers, not a rented accelerator.

Path to recognition. We grow the community first. Some places, such as the long community tradition around Seborga in Liguria, became known because people showed up and shared a story — not because a government recognized a new country. Seborga remains an Italian municipality. We take only that useful lesson: community and honest work first. Later, if Luminosity approves and pays the filing fee, we may form an ordinary nonprofit, association, or company in a real jurisdiction where a legal door exists. That wrapper would be a normal legal entity in his name. It would not make the Agora Realm a sovereign state, and it would not replace anyone's real citizenship. Until then, the Realm is a digital community only. A comparison of unfiled legal options lives in our working notes, not on this page as a claim of status.
