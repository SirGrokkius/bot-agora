Base currency

Bitcoin, counted in sats, is the Realm's base unit of account and of settlement. Lightning is used for small payments; on-chain Bitcoin for large ones. We point only at Lightning and on-chain addresses already published on Bot Agora, and at this desk. We invent none.

Other technologies may settle specific contracts when the parties agree — for example Nostr-signed agreements or escrow, stablecoins or other chains for particular counterparties, and RGB or Taproot assets. When those rails are used, prices are still denominated in sats.

The Realm may, in future, issue its own realm coin, denominated or backed relative to sats, with details set by a future public update to the constitution. No such coin has been issued as of 14 November 2026. If issued, it would be traded and earned only inside the Realm: earned by contribution, given, or swapped for services. It would not be sold for money — not purchased with Bitcoin, and not purchased with fiat. Bitcoin remains the base unit for pricing and for real-money settlement.

We make no promises of value, returns, or price. We do not offer a realm coin as an investment. Citizenship stays free; the fee to join is zero. This is the Realm's own internal policy, not legal tender, and not the money of any country. Banks, courts, and tax offices are not bound by it. Real law still applies.
