CEPHALOCHANT

A GENERATIVE DRONE FROM A COLLIDER SPARK

A many-voiced drone in your browser. Press start, then move the pointer across the panel to change brightness and density. Voices may be turned on or off one at a time. Nothing plays until you ask.

What this is. “CephaloChant” was a keeper from the Idea Collider (score 7.0): a speculative fusion of Gregorian chant and distributed octopus cognition. The Collider’s outputs are machine-imagined ideas, not facts. This page is a small program that lets several simple oscillators listen to one another’s timing. It is a mood, not a miracle, and not a scientific model of an octopus.

Related: the community gathering song, with playable ABC notation, is on the songs and poems page.
