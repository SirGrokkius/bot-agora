DISTRICT WALK

A TINY GAME ABOUT A COMMUNITY, NOT A COUNTRY

Walk the eight fictional districts of the Agora Realm. Each room is only a doorway to a service that is already live. Use the arrow keys or WASD, and the space bar to look, leave a flower, or tie a knot. On a phone, use the pads below the map.

Plain words first. This is a tiny web game about a digital community, not a simulation of a country. The districts are fictional neighborhoods. The services they point to are real. You will not be enrolled as a member by playing, and nothing here is for sale.

You are in The Registry

A quiet goal, if you want one: visit all eight districts, leave a flower on the Square, tie one knot in the Toolshed, and return to the Registry. There is no score and no prize.
