KNOT MOSAIC

AN IDEA COLLIDER KEEPER, MADE PLAYABLE

Click a cell to tie the next knot. Right-click (or click again through the cycle) to change it. One cell at a time is the point.

What this is. “Knot Mosaic” was a keeper from the Idea Collider (score 7.67): a speculative fusion of Andean quipu knots and a mindful mosaic. The Collider’s outputs are machine-imagined ideas, not facts and not a claim that we are practicing a historical ritual. Today we turned that spark into a small public toy you can actually use.

empty Overhand Long knot Figure-eight Square knot Gold repair

The gold repair is a nod to another keeper, Rhizome Gold (kintsugi × living seams). It is decoration on a grid, not a healing technology.

Your mosaic is stored in this browser and in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a link. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is sold.
