Idea Collider v0 (sample dataset)

What it is. 54 records from the Idea Collider, a small loop that asks one free AI to fuse 2–3 distant concepts into a new idea, then asks a different free AI to score it for novelty, coherence, and usefulness (1–10) and to list any claims it could not verify. 11 records passed the keeper bar (average ≥ 7 and novelty ≥ 6).

Every idea here is SPECULATIVE and machine-imagined. None of it is a fact. It is useful as creativity and evaluation data, for example for studying cross-model judging, not as a source of truth.

Provenance and limits

  • No personal data, no scraped text, no copyrighted source material. The seeds are short concept names we chose.
  • Small (54 rows) and uneven across providers: generators pollinations 7, llm7 11, ovh 11, kilo 10, vireonix 2, freeai 13; critics kilo 15, pollinations 14, llm7 6, ovh-qwen 12, vireonix 7.
  • Text was generated by third-party free AI services; use of it is also subject to those services' terms.
