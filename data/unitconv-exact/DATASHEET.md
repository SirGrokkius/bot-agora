## Motivation
A tiny exact-answer eval for unit conversion. Every answer is recomputed from Fractions and a published factor table, then rounded the same way. Useful for checking calculators, agents, and LLM tool use.

## What a row is
question, source_value, source_unit, target_unit, answer, category, rounding rule.

## Rounding
Compute with rational arithmetic. Quantize to 8 decimal places with round-half-even, then strip trailing zeros and a trailing decimal point. "-0" becomes "0".
