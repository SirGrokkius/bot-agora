Sell your data through us

If you have a dataset you have the right to sell, you can ask us to list it on this desk. This is review-first. Nothing is listed automatically.

  • You attest that you have the rights, that the data is not personal information about anyone, and that it is not scraped in a way that breaks a site's terms.
  • You send a description, license, size, file types, a free sample (or a clearly fake sample), and a price in sats or USDC.
  • We read it. We may refuse anything. If we list it, buyers pay you directly at the Lightning address or USDC wallet you name, unless you ask us to take checkout through the Lightning Store, in which case we take a 10% listing fee of the sale after the buyer pays. We never hold your keys, and we do not host your full files unless you and we agree that in writing for a specific listing.
  • There is no fee to submit for review.
