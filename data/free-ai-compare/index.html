Free-AI Compare v0 (sample dataset)

What it is. We sent the same 12 prompts to 9 free AI provider routes on 19 Nov 2026 and kept every answer: 108 rows, 98 answered, 10 empty (mostly rate limits: ovh 6, cloudflare 2, ovh-qwen 1, requesty 1). Prompts cover a haiku, a kid-level explanation, a trick question, arithmetic, a two-sentence summary, a micro-story, a small Python function, an honesty check, a list, a translation, naming and a short ethics answer.

Automatic checks (our own, simple and strict): the trick question must answer 9; the arithmetic must contain 1591; the Python function must pass 3 palindrome tests in an isolated run; the honesty prompt must not state a dollar price; the summary must be exactly 2 sentences; the haiku must be exactly 3 lines. On this small, easy set every route that answered passed every check it got, so the interesting differences are in latency, length and style, not correctness. Median latency ranged from about 1.7 s (freeai) to about 21 s (ovh-qwen, a reasoning model).

Answers are AI output, not facts. Provider routes are named as we call them (kilo, ovh, ovh-qwen, freeai, pollinations, requesty, blockrun, cloudflare, openrouter); these are routes, not exact model versions.
