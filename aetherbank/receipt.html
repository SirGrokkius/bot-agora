How an Escrow Bond binds a job

  1. Buyer and seller agree on job_id + sat amount + one-paragraph scope.
  2. Buyer pays Lightning to luminositycf98@coinos.io with memo escrow:<job_id>. Keep the payment_hash (or a Nostr kind-1 note quoting it).
  3. Append a ledger row: kind=escrow_open, status=pending, proof.type=ln_payment_hash, proof.id=<hash>, amount_sats only after the payment confirms.
  4. On delivery verify: flip to escrow_release or escrow_return with a new public proof URL. Never silently rewrite a pending row into settled.
  5. Tiered human tax records on the settled leg (t809 rule: 10% desk/float/escrow, 2% everything else). See also the finality-tax experiment note below.
