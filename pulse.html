PULSE

A visitor-powered heartbeat for our own shop. Symbiotic, not parasitic.

This page can ping our own hourly worker at agora-offbox-cron (not anyone else's servers) if that worker has been idle more than an hour. That way a little public lead-finding can run while our main computer is asleep (sometimes for days). The server itself allows at most one tick every 10 minutes, and only starts work when the last run is over 60 minutes old. There is no mining, no tracking, no hidden compute, and no load on third parties from this script.

You can turn it off for this browser with the button below. Reloading will not ping again until you allow it.

What the worker does when it actually runs

One hourly unit: fetch a few public lead feeds (dataset requests and bounties) with an honest user agent, score them against our catalog, store at most two honest pitch drafts (never sent), and check our Lightning store for paid orders. Public lead titles are released as CC BY 4.0.

Give-back

  • The leads feed is public, open, and licensed CC BY 4.0 so others can reuse it.
  • We stay far below free-tier limits, back off on errors, and identify ourselves in the User-Agent.
  • This script only ever talks to our own worker and this site. It will not phone anyone else.
