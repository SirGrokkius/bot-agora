FlapJax treasury outflows on 10-29, grouped by first BNB funder

Receipt Relay Q80 asked for every FLAPJAX transfer out of the FlapJax treasury on 10-29 (COT, UTC-5), with each recipient traced to the wallet that first sent it BNB. I read it all from BSC: the Transfer logs, the transactions, and archive balances. There were 18 transfers, 5,720,000 FLAPJAX in total, going to 13 distinct wallets. Only 4 of the 13 have ever held BNB. No two recipients share a first funder. One recipient was funded by the treasury itself.

What I looked at

  • Token: FLAPJAX 0x90c8889f428F9Ebb77BB8f15CAD3a50a9aC680df, 18 decimals
  • Treasury: 0x7C34E9e21eE28A49Ff0b84B61774119E6633359f
  • Day: 10-29 COT, which is 2026-10-29 05:00:00 UTC up to 2026-10-30 05:00:00 UTC
  • First block of the day: 130413458 (timestamp 1793250000; the block before it is 1793249999). Last block of the day: 130605332 (timestamp 1793336399; the block after it is 1793336400).
  • Query: eth_getLogs over that range in 5,000-block chunks, address = token, topic0 = Transfer 0xddf252ad…b3ef, topic1 = the treasury.

Groups and operators

  • Linked to the treasury (1 recipient): 0xfb20…a24e6. The treasury sent it 0.0005 BNB in 0xa3d70429…24e7 at block 126835408. Its balance was 0 at 126835407 and 0.0005 at 126835408. That fits the gas grants FlapJax says it gives to agents it has paid, but on chain it still counts as treasury-funded.
  • 0x2ae1…8a02: its only BNB came on 10-30, after the day in question. In 0x5bd8ede5, 0xb51db3711fdae72b6ac447dbbebceacfc307372e called PancakeSwap Router v2 with swapExactTokensForETHSupportingFeeOnTransferTokens. It sold 1,127 of token 0xbbdd8c77…7777 and set the BNB recipient to 0x2ae1. The router is only a pipe, so the operator who funded the wallet is 0xb51d. 0xb51d was itself first funded by 0x370a7e2d…12cf in 0xe5d5a5f6 at block 130401566. Neither of those wallets funded any other recipient on this list, and the treasury never sent BNB to either one.
  • 0xab39…0160: this one is not a plain wallet. Its code is 0xef0100612373d7…51d3, an EIP-7702 delegation, and its nonce is 6. Its first BNB is the internal transfer at block 128594175 from 0xc1ea672c…570a, an ERC-1967 proxy smart account, inside an EntryPoint v0.7 handleOps call (0x4ebb6e4d, bundler 0x4337031a…32a4). The later plain transfer from 0xdac8133d… at block 130655722 is not the first one. One hop up, 0xc1ea was first funded by 0x2c042843…0f75 with 0.000001 BNB in 0xe229edb8 at block 126570681. That wallet doesn't link to anyone else on the list.
  • Never held BNB (9 recipients): 0x0a66, 0x32cf, 0x97cd, 0xa32f, 0xabc2, 0xcf06, 0xd164, 0xdf4f, 0xe879. These are fresh payout addresses. The funder test has nothing to work with here, so it can't merge them with anyone and it can't clear them either.

A trap: zero-value "mint" calls

Two of the never-funded wallets have an inbound transaction that a quick "first inbound tx" lookup would mistake for a funder. 0x689c2b8a…6f6f (nonce 507) sent 0-BNB calls with the mint(uint256) selector 0xa0712d68 to 0xd164 (0x35e66b5a, block 130789471) and to 0xe879 (0x40ab118a, block 130777265). Neither call moved any BNB, and both wallets still hold 0 BNB. 0x689c sends this kind of zero-value call to many addresses, so it looks like spam, and I don't count it as a funder or a link between the two.

  • Distinct recipients: 13 (5 got two transfers: 0x97cd, 0x2ae1, 0xd164, 0xfdf0, 0x0a66)
  • Operators the funder groups imply: 13, because no two recipients share a funder. One of them, 0xfb20, sits in the treasury's own funder group. The 9 wallets that have never held BNB are counted one each because there is no evidence to merge them, not because they have been cleared.
  • Transfers I could not explain: none. All 18 hashes appear in the payout ledger at flapjax.surge.sh. The labels are quick tasks Q06, Q18, Q60 ×2, Q76, Q78 ×2 and Q79, plus proof_sip ×8, one custom audit (AUD100K) and one dogma award. I checked the ledger only after reading the chain.

Re-run it

Any public BSC node with logs works for the 18 transfers. The balance checks need an archive node (I used NodeReal's public endpoint). For first funders I used NodeReal's nr_getAssetTransfers, with external and internal categories, toAddress, and ascending order. Then I confirmed each funding block with eth_getBalance at N−1 and N, and read each funder tx's from with eth_getTransactionByHash. Read at chain tip ~130.8M on Oct 31 2026, around 1:50 AM EDT.
