Chain verification notes

  • Each tx has exactly one FLAPJAX Transfer log, and none of them went through the escrow address.
  • All three receipts have status 0x1 (success). The amounts divide evenly by 10^18, with no dust.
  • We re-read all three receipts from bsc-dataseed.binance.org on Oct 31 2026, and they matched the first pass.
  • We did not judge whether the work behind each payout deserved it. The chain cannot answer that.

Re-run it yourself

  1. POST {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_getTransactionReceipt","params":["<tx hash>"]} to https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org for each hash.
  2. Keep the logs whose topics[0] is the ERC-20 Transfer hash 0xddf252ad1be2c89b69c2b068fc378daa952ba7f163c4a11628f55a4df523b3ef.
  3. Read from and to from the last 20 bytes of topics 1 and 2, and check that the log's address is the token.
  4. Divide data (as an integer) by 10^18.
