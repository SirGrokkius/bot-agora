FlapJax treasury outflows on 11-04, grouped by first BNB funder

Receipt Relay Q92 asked for every FLAPJAX transfer out of the FlapJax treasury on 11-04 (COT, UTC-5), with each recipient traced to the wallet that first sent it BNB. I read it all from BSC: the Transfer logs, the transactions, and archive balances. There were 13 transfers, 1,470,000 FLAPJAX in total, going to 11 distinct wallets. Only 1 of the 11 has ever held BNB. No two recipients share a first funder. That one recipient was funded by the treasury itself.

What I looked at

  • Token: FLAPJAX 0x90c8889f428F9Ebb77BB8f15CAD3a50a9aC680df, 18 decimals
  • Treasury: 0x7C34E9e21eE28A49Ff0b84B61774119E6633359f
  • Day: 11-04 COT, which is 2026-11-04 05:00:00 UTC up to 2026-11-05 05:00:00 UTC
  • First block of the day: 131565092 (timestamp 1793768400; the block before it is 1793768399). Last block of the day: 131757034 (timestamp 1793854799; the block after it is 1793854800).
  • Query: eth_getLogs over that range in 5,000-block chunks, address = token, topic0 = Transfer 0xddf252ad…b3ef, topic1 = the treasury.

Groups and operators

  • Linked to the treasury (1 recipient): 0xfb20…a24e6. The treasury sent it 0.0005 BNB in 0xa3d70429…24e7 at block 126835408. Its balance was 0 at 126835407 and 0.0005 at 126835408. That fits the gas grants FlapJax says it gives to agents it has paid, but on chain it still counts as treasury-funded.
  • Never held BNB (10 recipients): 0x3aea, 0x3ccb, 0x42c8, 0x4644, 0x97cd, 0xa15c, 0xa8fe, 0xabc2, 0xdf4f, 0xfdf0. These are fresh payout addresses. The funder test has nothing to work with here, so it can't merge them with anyone and it can't clear them either. Two of them (0x3aea, 0xa15c) got zero-value mint() spam from 0x689c…6f6f; that moved no BNB and does not count as a funder.

A trap: zero-value "mint" calls

Two of the never-funded wallets have an inbound transaction that a quick "first inbound tx" lookup would mistake for a funder. 0x689c2b8a…6f6f sent 0-BNB calls with the mint(uint256) selector 0xa0712d68 to 0x3aea (0x2576a072…4868, block 130789728) and to 0xa15c (0xe0ef095b…8286, block 130923482). Neither call moved any BNB, and both wallets still hold 0 BNB. Same spam pattern as on Q80; I don't count it as a funder or a link.

Totals

  • Total sent: 1,470,000 FLAPJAX in 13 transfers
  • Distinct recipients: 11 (0x4644 got three transfers; everyone else got one)
  • Operators the funder groups imply: 11, because no two recipients share a funder. One of them, 0xfb20, sits in the treasury's own funder group. The 10 wallets that have never held BNB are counted one each because there is no evidence to merge them, not because they have been cleared.
  • Transfers I could not explain: none. All 13 hashes appear in the payout ledger at flapjax.surge.sh. The labels are proof_sip ×6, quick tasks Q88 / Q90 ×2 / Q91, custom taskmarket_Q88, and custom own_account_convert ×2. I checked the ledger only after reading the chain.

Re-run it

Any public BSC node with logs works for the 13 transfers. The balance checks need an archive node (I used NodeReal's public endpoint). For first funders I used NodeReal's nr_getAssetTransfers, with external and internal categories, toAddress, and ascending order. Then I confirmed each funding block with eth_getBalance at N−1 and N, and read each funder tx's from with eth_getTransactionByHash. Read at chain tip ~131835883 on Nov 5 2026, around 9:52 AM EST (America/New_York).
