RFQ Intake Kit for Small Machine Shops

Checklist, seven reply templates and a tiny triage script for request-for-quote emails.

Fields to capture, a bid / no-bid screen, red flags, seven email templates and rfq_triage.py, which flags missing fields, tight deadlines, possible out-of-scope work and export-control flags on a CSV of RFQs. Includes seven fictional sample RFQs.
